# AFTERZERO: The Complete Story

## The story in one breath

You are Ren. You wake in a sealed room in Station Zero with no memory and a floating object that becomes a device in your hand. The device shifts any room between PHYSICAL, INVERTED and ECHO, and it obeys laws you write. Juno, an engineer who has been hiding on the ceiling, and TICK, a small hint drone, keep you company. Meanwhile, recordings from Dr. Ilse Varga warn you never to activate the machine in the lab. But those recordings grow new sentences while you are away, one is dated "AFTER", and another is in your own voice, made eighteen years before you were born. A hatch behind the vault leads up into HALCYON, a city under a glass sky. Across twelve districts you learn four things: the city blamed you but its echoes blamed Varga, she flooded the reactor to buy eighteen years, the people are asleep rather than dead, and you were once her student and helped build the machine. Her echo at the top of the Signal Tower sends you home, where you take the power core from the Observer, a sentinel that learns every habit you show it, and wake the machine. The lab's walls turn into windows full of stars, and inside the machine, visible only in ECHO, floats a human brain whose pulse matches the Observer's eye. You choose to sever it or sustain it, or, if you have earned it, to let the Observer see you. Then the story moves to the Vessel, where one of seven crew members rewrote the core's first law at 03:12 and started the loop, and you must sit in the Captain's chair and name them. Every story number deals a new crew and a new truth, and the Story Studio lets you steer it.

---

## Characters

- **Ren (you).** Also called "Subject Zero". The station always calls you Ren, and you only choose how Ren looks. You form the theories, write the laws and make the final choice.
- **Juno Hale.** "Engineer, last one awake", who has been living on the ceiling. She guides you in the lab, and she is a voice on the radio everywhere else.
- **TICK.** A small round yellow drone with one glowing eye and a little rotor. He is your hint button and gives each hint in three steps: a nudge, a direction, then the answer.
- **Dr. Ilse Varga.** The scientist behind the lab and the machine, called "Director" in HALCYON. She speaks through recordings that rewrite themselves, and you meet her once, as an echo.
- **The Other ("???").** A violet, upside-down hologram that wears your face and speaks the game's first lines.
- **The Custodian.** A flickering teal hologram that keeps the archive and believes only proof.
- **The Observer.** A sleek white sentinel nearly three metres tall, with one glowing visor-eye. It hunts the Observation Hall and learns your habits.
- **The Device.** The object in your hand, which has a clipped, capital-letter voice of its own.
- **The brain.** The machine's "one occupant".
- **Kade Mori, Sign Fixer.** Alive in Neon Meridian. He still fixes the neon, though nobody comes.
- **Abena Osei, Reactor Chief.** An echo in the Drowned Reactor, still running her last shift.
- **Theo Lark, Botanist.** Alive in the Glass Forest (they/them). Theo has watered Varga's plants for eighteen years.
- **The Vessel crew.** Seven generated people, one per deck. Secretly, one is the culprit, one is the victim and one is a red herring.
- **The Captain.** An eighth generated name. You never meet the Captain and hear them only through a final log.

## Level by level

### PART ONE: THE PROLOGUE, STATION ZERO

Station Zero has four rooms around one hub:

- the **Antechamber** to the south, where you wake;
- the **Laboratory** in the centre, with a hidden passage behind its east wall;
- the **Archive** to the north, with the **Vault** across a gap;
- the **Observation Hall** to the west, where the Observer lives.

Three things on screen tell the story alongside the dialogue:

- **CURRENT UNDERSTANDING** is your objective. It rewrites itself with a glitch when the evidence changes. Old objectives stay in the list, struck through.
- **THEORY LOG** collects Ren's short first-person notes.
- **OPEN QUESTIONS** stay open until something answers them.

**Before you wake.** The title reads **AFTERZERO**: *"The world has rules. Nobody will tell you what they are."* Behind the menu, the camera circles a glowing object in a dim violet room, the room you are about to wake in. The character screen says: *"The station calls you Ren. Choose how you remember yourself."*

#### 1. The Antechamber

You are in a small sealed room about eight metres square, with a violet light strip along the floor. A sign on the back wall reads **SUBJECT HOLDING - CYCLE 0**. Debris lies scattered on the floor, and the north wall is dark, with no door. At head height floats a small white crystal inside a slowly turning violet wireframe shell, ringed by a faint halo.

Nothing tells you what to do. If you ask for help, the only answer is your own thought: *"Look closer at the floating thing."*

- **First touch.** The room flashes violet and gravity lets go. The debris drifts upward, and a jump becomes a slow float. *"Touching the object changed the room, not me. Gravity let go."*
- **Second touch.** The room turns over. You fall upward and land on the ceiling, and the object is now glowing in your left hand. It brings your first law words: GRAVITY, INVERTS IN, PHYSICAL, INVERTED and ECHO. Its first law slot already reads **GRAVITY INVERTS IN INVERTED**.
- **The reveal.** Upside-down writing on the ceiling is now readable under your feet: **YOU HAVE BEEN HERE BEFORE.** The dark north wall has vanished, because it exists in every state except INVERTED.

In the corridor beyond, also standing upside down, is a violet hologram wearing *your* face. It speaks:

> ???: "You finally learned how to move the room."
> ???: "Now learn why it was moving you."

When you walk toward it, it vanishes. You follow the corridor along its ceiling. Where it meets the Laboratory, normal gravity takes over and you drop to the floor. A door seals behind you, and your first objective appears: **"Escape the structure."** The reason it gives: *"I woke in a sealed room. Nothing else made sense."*

#### 2. The Laboratory

The Laboratory is a big room, 24 metres square and 10 metres tall, lit cool white. A sign reads **LAB 0 - FIELD CONTAINMENT**. In the centre stands **the Machine**: a large upright ring hovering over a metal base with an empty socket. There are three ways on:

- the north door, marked ARCHIVE, which is sealed;
- the west door, marked OBSERVATION in red, which is open;
- a faint rectangle on the east wall.

Someone lets go of the ceiling, drops to the floor and flips upright:

> JUNO HALE: "Don't scream. Please. It hears screaming."
> JUNO HALE: "You don't remember me. You never do. I'm Juno - engineer, last one awake. I've been living on the ceiling."
> JUNO HALE: "Take TICK. He's been waiting for you longer than I have."
> TICK: "HELLO. I AM TICK. IF YOU ARE STUCK, ASK ME. I WILL NOT LAUGH. MUCH."

**Recording 1.** Juno points you to the terminal by the crates:

> VARGA: "This is Dr. Ilse Varga. If the field is stable, you are hearing this from the lab terminal."
> VARGA: "Whatever you do, do not activate the machine."

Your objective becomes **"Find Dr. Ilse Varga."**

**The room in ECHO.** Shift the lab to ECHO and it turns pale mint and shows what happened here:

- glowing footprints leading to the Machine;
- the ghost of a shelf that is gone;
- writing on the walls: **THE ROOM REMEMBERS**, **STAND WHERE IT ISN'T** and **DO NOT ACTIVATE**.

**The wrong shadow.** A human shadow on the east wall moves the *opposite* way to you: left when you go right. In ECHO it stops being a stain and becomes a dark figure with a cyan outline, frozen where it last stood. Touch it and it is *"cold, and solid, and waiting for something you have not done yet."*

**The puzzle.** Do what the wall says and stand where the shadow isn't. Because the shadow mirrors you, standing on the opposite side of the room puts it over the door outline. Then shift to ECHO.

> "The shadow steps into the wall. The wall remembers a door."

A passage opens in the east wall.

#### 3. The Hidden Passage: Recording 0

This is a small violet room. A law word, **HELD MATTER**, hovers at waist height, and as you walk into it the device speaks: *"NEW LAW TOKEN. THE DEVICE CAN BE REWRITTEN."* There is also a terminal marked **RECORDING 0**, dated 14 March 2071. The voice on it is yours:

> YOU: "If you're hearing this, you made the shadow. Good."
> YOU: "Here's how it works: stand where it isn't, and let the room remember you there."
> YOU: "Things you take out of a memory don't survive leaving it. Unless you change the law."
> YOU: "You won't be born for eighteen years. Don't let that slow you down."

If you noticed the shadow moving, one more line plays: *"...wait. If I made the shadow - who was I controlling?"*

The recording is optional. Hearing it is one of three keys to the hidden ending.

#### 4. The Laboratory again: a word on the ceiling, a key made of memory

- **The ceiling word.** High over the lab, inside a metal frame hanging under the ceiling, glows **PERSISTS THROUGH**. Invert the lab, fall up onto the ceiling and walk over to it.
- **The memory key.** Juno says: *"The archive only believes proof. The key on that desk only exists in the echo. Make it real somehow."* In ECHO a key appears on the desk. Leave ECHO and *"The key comes apart in your hand and drifts back into memory."*
- **The Custodian.** Through the archive panel by the sealed door, the Custodian refuses you: *"There is no laboratory. There has never been a laboratory."* / *"You are standing in nothing. Bring me something that says otherwise."*
- **Your first law.** You write **HELD MATTER PERSISTS THROUGH PHYSICAL**. Take the key in ECHO and shift back to PHYSICAL, and this time the key survives.

If you show the key while still in ECHO, the Custodian sniffs: *"That key is a memory. Memories are not evidence."* In the real lab the panel reads "KEY ACCEPTED: LABORATORY 0", and the Custodian says only "..." The Archive door opens.

#### 5. The Archive

The Archive is a teal-lit room of shelves. A gap six metres wide cuts across its middle, and shelves hang from the ceiling over it, so you can't cross upside down. A sign on the far wall reads **NO RECORD OF LABORATORY**. The Custodian stands near the entrance, and its memory has changed:

> CUSTODIAN: "The laboratory. Yes. I remember it now."
> CUSTODIAN: "You came through that door before. You were older. You were carrying her."
> CUSTODIAN: "You told me to forget the laboratory, so you would have to prove it to yourself."
> CUSTODIAN: "You also told me to give you these."

It gives you **ECHOES** and **SOLIDIFY IN**, and your third law slot unlocks. The far sign changes to **RECORDS RESTORED: LABORATORY (2 VISITS)**. The game never says who "her" is.

**The puzzle.** In ECHO a ghostly bridge appears over the gap, with the words **MEMORY IS LOAD-BEARING**. You write **ECHOES SOLIDIFY IN ECHO** and walk across on a memory. If you leave the right state halfway, you fall, and *"The archive returns you to where it last remembered you."*

#### 6. The Vault

Past the gap are two shelves, the law word **ALL STATES** and a terminal: **RECORDING 2 - VAULT - DATED: AFTER**.

> VARGA: "This one is recorded after the activation. It is always after."
> VARGA: "You activate it. You always do. The way out of this place runs through the machine, and you are very good at finding ways out."
> VARGA: "I built the lab to keep it alive. I never told you what "it" was."
> VARGA: "When the walls become windows, look at the machine in the echo. Then decide."

If you have already switched the machine on, she also says *"You already did it, didn't you. I can hear the windows."*

Your objective becomes **"Stop the event you already caused."** The question "Where is Varga?" closes with the answer **"Not where. When."** Then Juno comes in:

> JUNO HALE: "Ren - behind the vault. A service hatch, and a ladder going up. A long way up."
> JUNO HALE: "If Varga is later, she went somewhere. I think she went up."

The screen fades, and you climb.

### PART TWO: HALCYON, the city on top

> JUNO: "Ren, that hatch goes up. All the way up. There is a whole city on top of Station Zero."
> JUNO: "They called it Halcyon. One glass sky, a million lights, and nobody left to switch them off."

HALCYON has four chapters of three districts each, twelve in all. Your device now holds all twelve Station Zero words, including OBSERVER and FREEZES IN. Juno's body never appears up here. She is a voice that answers after each recorder, note and person you find. (You can also start straight here by choosing HALCYON from the chapter menu.)

#### How a district works

- **The space.** Each district is one open map under a glass dome 22 metres up. You arrive at the south end beside a **memory beacon** that saves your progress.
- **The gate.** The north gate stays locked until you complete the district's scene: a recorder on a roof, a note on a wall, a public screen to read twice, or a person to meet.
- **Water.** Many districts are cut in two by a canal with a "BRIDGE CLOSED" sign. Fall in, and you return to your last checkpoint.
- **Drones.** Smaller copies of the Observer pace the far side. They watch only the ground ahead, in a narrow cone. Their eye turns from blue to amber to red-orange as they notice you. If one catches you, TICK says: *"IT ONLY WATCHES THE FLOOR. OR YOU COULD ASK IT TO HOLD STILL. POLITELY. WITH A LAW."*
- **Memory fragments.** Up to three glowing crystals per district: one on a rooftop, one in a back street, one on the far bank (or, in dry districts, near the north end). They are optional, and each holds one small story.

Every obstacle has more than one answer:

- **A scene on a roof:**
  - *Dome walk:* shift to INVERTED, fall up onto the glass sky, walk until you're over the roof, then shift back and drop.
  - *Memory stairs:* climb seven remembered steps in ECHO, using ECHOES SOLIDIFY IN ECHO.
  - *Zero gravity:* write GRAVITY FREEZES IN a state and float up.
- **Water:** solidify the remembered bridge in ECHO, or cross it upside down on the dome.
- **Drones:** stay behind buildings, go up onto the dome (they can't see you while you're inverted), or freeze them with OBSERVER FREEZES IN.

#### Chapter 1: Neon Meridian, "The City Under Glass"

*"A city that never turns its lights off, under a sky made of glass."* Rain falls on pink and cyan neon: NOODLES 24H, DREAM CLINIC, KARAOKE, LUCKY 8. Cars with red tail-lights are parked along the avenue.

**District 1: Meridian Square.** This district has no water and no drones. On the side of a tower block, pink paint says **FOLLOW THE PINK LIGHTS**. On its roof, Varga's recorder plays:

> VARGA: "If you are hearing this, you came up the long way. Good. The Observer cannot see past its own floors, so stay high when you can. I am going to the Signal Tower. Follow the pink lights."
> JUNO: "Stay high. Roofs, overpasses, the glass itself if you have to. I can steer you."

**District 2: Noodle Row.** Across a canal, past one drone, stands a giant public screen:

> "WANTED: SUBJECT ZERO - FOR THE ZERO EVENT - DO NOT APPROACH"

Shift to ECHO, and the screen remembers differently:

> "WANTED: ILSE VARGA - FOR TRYING TO STOP IT"
> YOU: "The screen says the city hunted me. The echo says it hunted Varga. Someone rewrote who the villain was."

**District 3: Glass Market.** In a neon-lit stall past the water is a living man:

> KADE: "Hey - hey! Don't run. Nobody runs on my street. You'll scare the signs."
> KADE: "Kade. I fix the neon. Twelve years, every night, and not one customer. Feels honest work, though."
> KADE: "The woman with the grey coat? Varga. She came through crying and laughing at the same time. Took the old service lift to the reactor."
> KADE: "Told me if a kid with a glowing thing in their hand comes by, I should say: the lights are a map."
> JUNO: "The lights are a map. Ren, the pink ones run north, toward the reactor stack."

**What the fragments remember.** A city frozen mid-life:

- a shop sign reading *"CLOSED FOR THE ZERO EVENT. BACK SOON. The tape is eighteen years old."*;
- a police log, *"Subject Zero sighted near the square."*, crossed out and corrected to *"Varga."*;
- a karaoke booth still playing a love song to nobody;
- a ticket for *"the last train out of Meridian. The train never left."*;
- *"A note under a bench: if you are reading this, the lights still work. Good. Leave them on."*

#### Chapter 2: The Drowned Reactor, "Where the Water Came In"

*"Halcyon's heart flooded the night the cycle began."* Steam, teal fog, orange alarm light, tanks and pipes, and black water through every district.

> JUNO: "The reactor. It is flooded, Ren. Halls full of black water and the lights are still on underneath."
> TICK: "THE WATER IS VERY DEEP AND I AM VERY SMALL. BRIDGES PLEASE."

**District 1: Turbine Floor.** The shift log sits on a pump-house roof across the water, under the words **PRESSURE IS A PROMISE**. You cross the water and climb in one go by walking the dome.

> REACTOR SHIFT LOG - 03:04: "Chief Osei on shift. Pressure is climbing for no reason I can find. Someone is in the control deck rewriting the core's first law. The doors will not open for me. They open for her."
> JUNO: "Her. Osei saw someone rewriting the core. Varga, or someone with Varga's keys."

**District 2: The Sump.** Past the water, a note is fixed to a pump:

> VARGA: "I opened the flood valves myself. It was the only way to slow the core down. Osei, I am sorry. Ren, if you read this: the water bought us eighteen years. Do not waste them."
> JUNO: "Eighteen years. That number again. Recording 0 was eighteen years before you were born."

**District 3: Control Deck.** One drone patrols here. In the control deck you find Abena Osei, now only a glowing echo still running her last shift:

> OSEI: "Pressure at nine-forty and climbing. Seal the lower gallery. Seal it!"
> OSEI: "Who is that? You are not on my shift. Nobody is on my shift anymore."
> OSEI: "She opened the valves. She saved the city and drowned my reactor in the same breath. I would have done the same."
> OSEI: "Go up the core well. The forest is above us. She went to the cabin. She always went to the cabin."
> JUNO: "That was an echo. Osei has been saying that to an empty room for eighteen years."

**What the fragments remember:**

- a hard hat scratched *"OSEI. Under it, smaller: and her crew, every one."*;
- a pressure gauge stuck at nine-forty;
- a thermos of tea, *"still warm. That should not be possible."*;
- a repair tag signed by Varga *"with a little drawing of a fish"*;
- graffiti on a tank: *"THE WATER BOUGHT US TIME. SPEND IT WELL."*

#### Chapter 3: The Glass Forest, "The Garden That Kept Growing"

*"A forest under a dome, still glowing, still growing, still waiting."* It is dusk, with fireflies, moss and glowing trees. Some of the trees are only memories of trees: outlines that appear in ECHO and never turn solid.

> JUNO: "Oh. Ren. Look up. Trees. Real ones. They glow."
> TICK: "SOME OF THE TREES ARE MEMORIES OF TREES. DO NOT LEAN ON THOSE."

**District 1: The Canopy.** On top of a great bark platform seven metres up, ringed by tall trees and marked **EVERYTHING HERE WAS PLANTED TO OUTLIVE US**:

> GARDEN LOG - DAY 6570: "Theo again. The glow-roots are spreading toward the cabin. Dr. Varga still has not come back. I water her plants anyway. Somebody should be here when she does."

Day 6570 is eighteen years.

**District 2: The Still Pond.** Across the water stands a memorial wall of bark:

> "IN MEMORY OF THE PEOPLE OF HALCYON - LOST IN THE ZERO EVENT"
> TICK: "STONE REMEMBERS WHAT IT WAS ASKED TO SAY. ECHO REMEMBERS WHAT IT MEANT."

In ECHO it reads:

> "THEY ARE NOT LOST - THEY ARE ASLEEP - WAKE THEM WHEN IT IS SAFE"
> YOU: "The memorial says Halcyon's people died. The echo says they are asleep. Varga did not kill this city. She put it to sleep."

**District 3: The Cabin Road.** Past one drone, at the cabin, someone is alive:

> THEO: "Stop there. Hands where the roots can see them. ...Oh. Oh, it is you. You are smaller than she said."
> THEO: "You were her student, you know. Before. You helped her build the machine. Then you asked her to make you forget."
> THEO: "She is at the top of the Signal Tower. Or what is left of her is."
> JUNO: "Her student. Ren, you built the machine. That is why it knows your face."

**What the fragments remember:**

- *"A seed packet labelled for Ren, in Varga's handwriting. Inside: one seed, already sprouting."*;
- Theo's watering schedule, *"Every day for eighteen years. Not one day missed."*;
- *"A drawing of the machine, in a child's hand. The child has drawn a heart inside it."*;
- a trail marker to the cabin with *"HURRY"* added.

#### Chapter 4: The Signal Tower, "Last Light"

*"The tallest thing in Halcyon, and the Observer knows you are coming."* A storm beats on the glass, red light everywhere, masts and neon rods on every roof.

> JUNO: "The tower. Storm on the glass, red lights everywhere. And Ren - it is here. The Observer. It got here first."
> "It has been waiting at the top of the world. It knows the way you climb."

This chapter is the stealth finale: two, then three, then four drones, the most anywhere in HALCYON.

**District 1: Storm Deck.** On a relay hut marked **SAY IT ONCE AND THE WHOLE SKY HEARS**:

> VARGA - RELAY LOG: "The broadcast that starts the cycle goes out from this tower every zero hour. I can stop it from here. I can also stop it from Station Zero, the right way, with a choice instead of a switch. I am not brave enough to choose for everyone."
> JUNO: "She could have ended it here. She did not. She left it for someone to choose."

**District 2: Antenna Field.** Past the water and three drones:

> VARGA - TO REN: "Ren. You asked me to make you forget so that you could choose honestly. Go back down to Station Zero. Install the core. Wake the machine and look inside it. Then decide. I will be the light at the top of the tower when you need me."

**District 3: Last Landing.** At the top of the tower, past four drones, Varga herself waits in a lit alcove, as an echo:

> VARGA: "There you are. You climbed it. You always climbed it, every cycle, and every cycle I wondered if this would be the one."
> VARGA: "Do not grieve me. I am an echo of a woman who made one good decision and one terrible one, the same night."
> VARGA: "The people of Halcyon are asleep below the forest. The machine keeps them dreaming. It keeps someone else alive, too. You will see."
> VARGA: "Go home, Ren. Station Zero. The lift behind me goes all the way down. Whatever you choose, choose it awake."
> JUNO: "The lift goes down to the lab. Come home, Ren. I will meet you there."

The gate behind her reads **LIFT - STATION ZERO**.

**What the fragments remember:**

- a badge reading *"R. VARGA-ZERO. The last name is scratched almost through."*, which the game never explains;
- *"A cracked visor with a reflection still burned into it: two people, one of them you."*;
- a map with an X at the top: *"here is where I chose not to choose."*;
- *"A letter: Ren, you will hate me for this, and then you will understand, and then you will forgive me. In that order."*;
- a lamp *"set to switch off the moment you leave."*

### PART THREE: HOME TO STATION ZERO

#### 7. Home

A card reads **STATION ZERO / Home / "The lift opens into the laboratory"**.

> JUNO: "You are back. Same lab. It looks different now that we know what it is for."
> JUNO: "The core is in the observation hall. The machine is waiting. Varga said look inside it before you choose."

TICK adds: *"THE MACHINE NEEDS A CORE. THE HALL HAS ONE."* The socket is empty: *"Something round and heavy belonged here."*

#### 8. The Observation Hall

The hall is long and dark, lit by red strips, with eleven tall pillars dividing it into three aisles. By the door a sign says **OBSERVATION HALL - DO NOT RUN**. At the far end the amber **POWER CORE** glows on its pedestal. In ECHO the far wall reads **IT ONLY KNOWS WHAT YOU TEACH IT**.

The Observer patrols. A faint cone shows where it is looking, and its eye shows its mood:

- pale blue when calm;
- amber when suspicious;
- coral-red when hunting;
- lavender when frozen.

It remembers where you hide. Use the same pillar a second time and *"It pauses by the pillar."* A third time and *"It looks directly at your pillar."* Later it simply *"walks straight to the pillar you keep hiding behind."*

**It learns.** Every habit you show it becomes a lesson, announced in one line:

- **Hiding on the ceiling** for too long: *"It tilts its head upward, toward the ceiling."*
- **Freezing it with a law** too often (it works twice and fails the third time): *"It moved. It should not be able to move."*
- **Running** too much: *"It is listening for your footsteps now."*
- **Always crossing by the same aisle:** *"It waits where you usually cross."*
- **Getting caught** twice: *"It lingers longer than before."*

If it catches you, the screen fades, a voice says *"It remembers you."*, and you are back at the door with the core on its pedestal. So you vary everything:

- walk the ceiling until it learns to look up;
- read its patrol route in ECHO as a trail of light;
- freeze it with OBSERVER FREEZES IN while that still works;
- never give it the same habit twice.

You carry the core home.

(The hall is open from the very start, so a bold player can steal the core before ever going up.)

#### 9. The Machine wakes

> "The core seats itself. The machine is waiting."
> JUNO: "It's ready. Varga said don't. I say... your call."

Use it once and Varga's warning plays again, and the device says *"USE AGAIN TO ACTIVATE."* Use it again:

> "The field opens."
> "The walls are not walls."
> YOU: "Stars. It's not a lab. It's moving."

The inner skin of two walls vanishes and leaves **glass**. Outside are stars and a violet planet, slowly turning. The lab's light begins to breathe amber, and the sign over the door now reads **VESSEL AFTERZERO - LIFE SUPPORT: 1 OCCUPANT**.

Replay Recording 1, and it has grown:

> VARGA: "If you have already activated it, I am sorry."
> VARGA: "That last sentence was not there the first time you listened. I added it later. Much later."

The Observer stops hunting. It walks out of its hall and stands in the doorway, its eye pink and pulsing. Juno says: *"Stars. I'd forgotten what they look like. Look at the machine in the echo - something's inside."*

#### 10. The brain and the doorway

You shift to ECHO. Inside the ring floats a pink **human brain**, glowing from within.

> "Inside the ring: a human brain, suspended, lit from within."
> YOU: "The machine was built around it."
> "Cables surface from the floor and run west - toward the hall."
> "The Observer stands in the doorway. It does not hunt you now."
> "Its eye pulses at the same rate as the brain."

Your last objective appears: **"Decide what the catastrophe is holding back."** The reason it gives: *"The machine keeps something alive. Stopping it may be worse."*

The Custodian now says: *"It was a life-support system. For one occupant. You have met him."* Juno says goodbye in her own way: *"Whatever you choose, I'm glad it was you this time."*

If you choose to **face the Observer** in the doorway:

> "It looks at you the way you look at a mirror."
> YOU: "It learned how I hide. How I run. How I think."
> YOU: "It was learning to be me."

#### 11. The final choice

You use the Machine once more:

> **THE MACHINE.** "The machine keeps one occupant alive. The occupant keeps the field alive. The field keeps something else shut."

- **SEVER:** "Pull the core. End the field."
- **SUSTAIN:** "Leave it running. Take Varga's place."
- **MIRROR:** "Let the Observer see you." This appears only if you have earned it (see Endings).
- **NOT YET:** walk away and come back later.

Afterwards, an end screen shows your time, how many of 15 discoveries you made, how often you were caught and how many lessons you taught the Observer. It offers: **CONTINUE TO THE VESSEL**: *"Eight decks. A crew that vanished. One of them started the loop."*

### PART FOUR: THE VESSEL

**How it fits.** The Vessel plays as the next chapter on the same ship. The lab's own sign already called it VESSEL AFTERZERO. But the Vessel never checks which ending you chose, and its truth changes with every story number (the seed). Each run is one *cycle* among many. Its culprit is always a crew member at the core at 03:12. That sits a little uneasily beside HALCYON, which pointed toward "her" and Varga's keys.

#### The shared frame

**Getting aboard.** There are three ways in:

- the button at the end of the prologue;
- THE VESSEL on the chapter menu ("Eight generated decks, a crew, a culprit");
- the **CYCLE 43** button after finishing one Vessel.

Your device has all three law slots, and TICK is at your shoulder. Coming straight from the prologue, you keep everything you earned. Juno is never seen on board; she is only a voice on the radio.

**The mystery.** The ship is caught in a repeating cycle. It began at the **Zero Event, at 03:12**, when someone at the core rewrote the ship's first law. The crew keep hinting at the repeats: *"Cycle forty-two. You're late."* / *"That matches the last forty-one answers."*

Each story number secretly picks:

- a **culprit** (never the crew of Decks 1 or 2, so you meet a few people before suspecting anyone);
- a **victim**;
- a **motive**;
- a **method**;
- a **twist**;
- a **red herring**, an innocent person whose official record looks damning.

Every innocent person's echo clears them. Only the culprit's echo contradicts their record.

**Your board.** The journal holds THE QUESTION (*"Someone on this ship rewrote the core's first law at 03:12 and started the loop... The echoes don't always agree."*). It also has a SUSPECTS list, where each person is marked UNKNOWN, MET, ALIBI CONFIRMED or ECHO CONTRADICTS, and a log of everything you've read.

**How a deck plays.** Every deck has four sectors. Each sector is a chain of rooms with turning corridors and ends at a glowing gate. Each sector holds one story room and one or more puzzle rooms. The four story beats on Decks 1 to 7 are:

1. **Arrival.** The crew member's **LOG 1**. For example: *"Log. {name}. Nobody reads these. I write them anyway, the way you leave a light on for someone who isn't coming."* TICK then remarks on the deck.
2. **The alibi.** **LOG 2**: where they were on the night of the Zero Event. Innocents say something like *"I remember the lights going soft, like someone exhaling."* The culprit always gives the too-careful version: *"Zero Event, my statement: {place}, alone, all night. The cameras will say the same. I made sure they would."*
3. **The record.** TICK: *"A RECORD ON THE WALL. RECORDS HAVE ECHOES."* Read the plaque, shift to ECHO and read it again:
   - **Innocent:** the echo matches, and names someone who was with them.
   - **Red herring:** "LOCATION UNKNOWN - CAMERAS WIPED", but the echo shows them *"CRYING - NOT ALONE"*.
   - **Culprit:** **"ECHO: {NAME} AT THE CORE - 03:12 - HANDS ON THE FIRST LAW"**.
4. **The encounter.** Juno: *"Someone's in there. Ren - be gentle."* The crew member is there, solid if alive, a glowing hologram if not.
   - **Their greeting depends on personality.** Warm: *"Sit, sit - no, you can't sit, the chairs are on the ceiling."* Dry: *"Cycle forty-two. You're late."* Nervous: *"I thought you were the watcher. You move like it now."* Formal: *"Subject Zero. I am authorised to speak with you..."* Dreamy: *"I dreamt you would come through that door. Upside down, in the dream."*
   - **Innocents** deny it, point at someone else (rightly or not) and say *"Whoever did it - they loved someone. That's the only reason anyone breaks a ship."*
   - **The culprit** ends with **"Go on to the bridge. Sit in the chair. Then judge me."**

Story rooms never lock you in. Only the very last gate on the Bridge refuses you.

**Puzzles on board.** Every room has three states, and your device holds three laws.

- **Pits** too wide to jump. Each pit is built for one way across:
  - a walkway painted on the ceiling (invert);
  - a remembered bridge (solidify echoes);
  - a gold outline bridge (solidify hard light);
  - an empty gap you drift across in zero gravity.
- **Time sweepers**, rotating beams: jump them, or freeze time.
- **Watched halls**, where a sentinel paces among pillars: hide, walk the ceiling (*"INVERTED KEEPS YOU OUT OF ITS SIGHT"*), or freeze it.
- **Exits:**
  - an open door;
  - a door on the ceiling;
  - a 4-metre ledge;
  - a **memory key** that exists only in ECHO, for a lock that reads only real matter;
  - a **power core** hung from the ceiling or set on a high shelf.

Falling or being caught is gentle: a fade, and you're back at the checkpoint.

#### Deck 1: Hydroponics, "The Garden Deck"

This is the Botanist's deck, and it teaches **gravity**: ceiling walkways, ceiling doors, cores hung upside down. Rooms include the Nursery Ring, the Root Vault, the Mist Gallery and Greenhouse 3. The motto reads **"EVERYTHING HERE WAS PLANTED TO OUTLIVE US"**. Juno's first radio call may be *"Juno here. I'm on the radio now - the hatch behind you sealed, but I can hear you."*

#### Deck 2: Cryo Bay, "The Sleeping Deck"

This is the Cryo Technician's deck, and it teaches **echoes**: memory bridges, memory stairs and memory keys, among Pod Row A, the Frost Corridor and the Glass Coffins. The motto reads **"SLEEP IS A PROMISE TO WAKE"**.

#### Deck 3: Observatory, "The Weightless Deck"

This is the Navigator's deck, and it teaches **zero gravity**: you drift over gaps and float up to ledges in the Star Dome, the Orrery and the Telescope Well. The motto reads **"WE KNEW WHERE WE WERE GOING. WE NEVER ASKED WHY"**. From here on, anyone you meet could be the culprit.

#### Deck 4: Engineering, "The Clockwork Deck"

This is the Chief Engineer's deck. It brings **time sweepers** and a new law word, **TIME**, floating in the first room. TICK: *"A NEW SUBJECT: TIME. TIME FREEZES IN (A STATE) STOPS EVERYTHING THAT SWEEPS."* The motto reads **"EVERY MACHINE KEEPS TIME. THIS ONE KEEPS IT PRISONER"**.

#### Deck 5: Medbay, "The Watched Deck"

This is the Medic's deck, and the **sentinels** arrive: pale, one-eyed copies of the Observer pacing Triage, Quarantine and Surgery 2. The motto reads **"DO NO HARM. OBSERVE EVERYTHING"**.

#### Deck 6: Crew Quarters, "The Home Deck"

This is the Counselor's deck. A new word, **HARD LIGHT**, floats in the first room. TICK: *"THE GOLD OUTLINES CAN CARRY WEIGHT IF YOU TELL THEM TO."* Gold bridges and stairs run through the Mess Hall, the Bunk Ring, the Shrine and the Music Room. The motto reads **"WE WERE A FAMILY FOR ELEVEN YEARS"**.

#### Deck 7: Archive Core, "The Remembering Deck"

This is the Archivist's deck. **Everything comes back at once**, with no favourite, in the Deep Stacks, the Memory Forge and the Redaction Room. The motto reads **"NOTHING IS FORGOTTEN. SOME THINGS ARE FILED ELSEWHERE"**.

#### Deck 8: The Bridge, "The Last Deck"

The Bridge is the same in every run. Its motto reads **"WHOEVER SITS HERE DECIDES WHAT HAPPENED"**.

> JUNO: "The bridge. I can see your signal on the map and I still don't believe it."
> JUNO: "Captain {name} locked the chair from the inside. The ship wants a verdict before it opens."

- **Sector 1.** *CAPTAIN'S LOG - FINAL:* "The crew is asleep, or gone, or both. Someone on this ship {method}. I know who. I will not say it - the ship has to hear it from someone who earned it." TICK: *"LAST DECK. I AM NOT CRYING. I DO NOT HAVE THE HARDWARE."*
- **Sector 2.** *VARGA - NOTE TO SUBJECT ZERO:* "Ren. If you made it here you have seen every deck and every lie. The chair asks one question. Answer it with what you know, not with what you fear." Juno: *"Varga wrote that to you. Not to me. Interesting."*
- **Sector 3.** *ZERO EVENT - RECONSTRUCTION:* "03:12. The core's first law was rewritten. One person was at the core. Everyone else has an echo that says where they were. Check your board." Juno: *"Look at who doesn't have one."*
- **Sector 4, the verdict.** After three last puzzle rooms, all seven crew stand as silent holograms in a ring around a chair. Juno: *"This is it. The chair. Check your log before you sit."* Try the gate first, and she says: *"The gate won't open for a guess. Sit in the chair first."*

#### The chair

You sit. **THE CHAIR: "Who rewrote the first law at 03:12?"** All seven crew are listed. Each shows what you found: "echo contradicts the record", "alibi confirmed" or "no echo found". Name the wrong person and the ship plays back their innocent echo, then lets you try again. Name the right one and the chair unlocks (see Endings).

### PART FIVE: STORY STUDIO

The fourth chapter card reads **STORY STUDIO**: "A vessel story made from your wishes". The panel says: *"Tell the vessel what kind of story you want. The crew, the clues, the rooms and the culprit are built around it, offline."* You fill in six wishes, press **CREATE STORY** and pick a save slot. What follows is a normal eight-deck Vessel built around them.

There is one rule: **a wish replaces only what it names.** Everything else stays exactly as that story number would have made it.

- **Title.** Your own name for the story. It appears wherever the save is described, not inside the game.
- **Mood.** This changes every crew member's personality, and so their greeting to you. Thriller makes the crew dry, nervous or formal. Horror makes them mostly nervous. Mystery makes them formal, dry or dreamy. Hopeful makes them mostly warm. Horror and Thriller also darken the decks.
- **Why the time loop exists.** Choose the motive: keep someone alive, hide where the ship arrived, undo a terrible mistake, or because the Observer promised a way home.
- **The final twist.** This is the title of your winning ending: You helped start it / The watcher is a lost crew member / Juno remembers everything / The stars outside are fake.
- **Crew names.** Up to eight first names, given deck by deck. The eighth goes to the Captain. Your device's own voice speaks the names you type.
- **Story number.** *"The same number and wishes always tell the same story."*

You can't wish for who the culprit is, who the victim is, the method, the red herring, or who survives. Those come from the story number. The eight decks themselves never change.

For example, take story 4242 with a Horror mood, the names Aria, Dev and Mina, and the twist "Juno remembers everything". Ximena becomes Aria, Uma becomes Dev and Kalani becomes Mina. Kofi now greets you dreamily, Dev and Yusuf nervously, and the decks are darker. Kofi is still the culprit. But the winning screen now ends: *"Juno has never once been reset. She has lived every cycle, end to end, and every time she let you rediscover it alone - because the one time she told you first, you chose wrong."*

## Endings

### The Prologue (at the Machine)

**SEVERED.** You choose SEVER.
> You pull the core. The brain goes dark between one pulse and the next. In the doorway, the Observer folds to the floor like a coat with no one in it. The windows go black. The ship stops moving. For a long, quiet moment, nothing happens. Then, far beneath the station, something that was being held shut opens its eyes.

**SUSTAINED.** You choose SUSTAIN.
> You leave the machine running. The brain keeps its slow rhythm. The ship keeps moving. You find a terminal and begin to record: "This is Dr. Ilse Varga..." - no. Your own name. Then you stop. Somewhere behind you, in a sealed room, someone wakes up and sees a glowing object for the first time. You go to stand in the corridor, upside down, and wait to tell them how to move the room.

The text strongly suggests you are about to become the Other from the first room, though the game never says so in words.

**OBSERVED (hidden).** You choose MIRROR. The option appears only if you have done all three of these:

- **faced the Observer** in the doorway;
- **heard Recording 0** in the hidden passage;
- **brought the Custodian a real key** and spoken to it in the Archive, so that it remembers.

> You walk to the doorway and stand where the Observer can see you. You do not hide. It has learned how you hide, how you run, which laws you lean on. Now it learns the last thing: that you chose to be seen. The cables slacken. The brain's rhythm changes to match yours. There are two of you in the doorway. Then there is one, and the station knows your name.

Being caught by the Observer is never an ending. It just sends you back to the hall's door.

### The Vessel (in the chair)

- **A wrong name is not an ending.** Each of the six innocents has their own version of this: *"You name {name}. The ship plays back {first}'s echo, calm and complete: {first} was in {place} all night."* / *"The lights soften. Somewhere below, a hatch unseals. The cycle notes your answer and gives you another chance."* The gate stays shut, and you can sit again as often as you like. Every guess is counted.
- **The right name** opens an ending screen. It is titled by the twist and carries the motive and the twist text. There are four twists:
  - **YOU HELPED:** "The loop has two signatures. One is {culprit}'s. The other is yours. Cycle one, you agreed to all of this - and asked to forget..."
  - **THE WATCHER'S NAME:** "The Observer is not a machine. It is {victim}, uploaded on the night of the Zero Event... It learned you because it missed you."
  - **SHE REMEMBERS:** "Juno has never once been reset. She has lived every cycle, end to end..."
  - **THE WINDOWS:** "The stars outside are an echo. The vessel stopped moving long ago..."

  The motive paragraph is one of four:
  - *to keep {victim} alive*, because the machine keeps one occupant alive while the cycle runs;
  - *to hide where we had arrived*;
  - *to undo a mistake*, one navigation error;
  - *because the Observer promised a way home*.

  Four twists times four motives makes sixteen possible right endings.
- **Afterwards** the screen shows your time, how many verdicts you gave, how many crew you met and the story number. Choose **CYCLE 43** ("A new vessel, a new crew, a new truth") to begin again with a new crew. Or choose **STAY ON THE BRIDGE**: the chair now only says *"The chair has its answer."*, and walking through the last gate brings the ending back.
